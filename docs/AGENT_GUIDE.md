# Touchpoint Widget — Agent Guide

> This guide reflects exactly what the widget can do today. It's kept up to
> date as the widget changes — if something here doesn't match what you see
> on screen, let whoever maintains the widget know so it can be fixed.

## What it's for

The Touchpoint Widget is how you log a call with a client, right from their
Maintenance Offer in the CRM. It walks you through a couple of quick
questions about the call, and — depending on what kind of call it was — a
short form to capture the details. When you submit, the call is saved and
the widget closes on its own.

## Before you start

Have the call first, then open the widget and fill it in while it's fresh —
most of what you're asked comes straight from things the client said, so
there's nothing to prepare in advance beyond paying attention during the
call.

## Step 1 — Tell us about the call

You'll always start on the same screen with two quick questions:

- **Conversation option** — what kind of conversation this was supposed to
  be. Pick the one that matches why you called (see the table below for what
  each one leads to). The list of options you see can be shorter depending
  on the client's current status — for example, Re-engagement only shows up
  for clients who've gone quiet or already been marked lost, not for
  clients actively using the service.
- **Call outcome** — did the client pick up? Connected, No answer, Busy, or
  Wrong number.

**If the client didn't pick up** (anything other than Connected), you'll
just be asked for a **next follow-up date** — when you plan to try this
client again. Fill that in and submit. You're done; there's nothing else to
log for a call that didn't happen.

**If the call connected**, hitting **Next** takes you to a short form based
on the conversation option you picked:

| If you picked...           | You'll see...                                |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Intro**                  | The Intro form — see below.                  |
| **Post-service feedback**  | The Post-Service Feedback form — see below.  |
| **Active Client Feedback** | The Active Client Feedback form — see below. |
| **Re-engagement**          | Opens the Re-engagement form — see below.    |
| **Churned Feedback**       | Opens the Churned form — see below.          |

## Step 2a — Intro calls

Use this when it's a first conversation introducing the client to the
service. Collect:

- **How the client reacted** — Interested, Neutral, Not now, or Refused.
- **If they refused** — why (Price, No Need, Timing, Competitor, No Trust,
  Other), and how firm that refusal felt (Hard or Soft).
- **Which services you discussed** — pick every one that came up: Oil & PM
  Services, Tire Services, Mechanical Repairs, Inspections, Wash &
  Cleaning, Parking, Roadside Assistance, Dealership.
- **A short summary** of how the conversation went, in your own words.
- **Next follow-up date.**

## Step 2b — Post-Service Feedback calls

Use this when you're following up after a service was completed, to collect
the client's feedback on it.

- **How satisfied they were** with the service — Good, Neutral, or Bad.
- **Whether they'd use the service again** — Yes, Maybe, or No.
- **Which service requests this feedback is about, and a rating for each**
  — you'll see a table of this client's recent service requests; check the
  box next to every one the client is giving feedback on, and pick a Shop
  Rating and an Agent Rating (1 to 5) right there in that row. Different
  requests can have been handled by different shops or agents, so rate
  each one on its own — there's no single overall rating for the whole
  call. If a request already has a rating from an earlier call, it shows
  up pre-filled — just leave it as-is or update it if the client's
  feedback has changed. You must select at least one request, and both
  ratings are required for every request you check. If there are a lot of requests,
  use the page controls above the table to look through them.
- **Next follow-up date.**

## Step 2c — Active Client Feedback calls

Use this for a general check-in with a client who's actively using the
service.

- **Overall experience** — Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied,
  or Very Unsatisfied.
- **What they like** about the service — pick everything that applies:
  Price / Savings, Discounts, Promotions, Convenient Locations, Shop
  Network, Service Availability, Fast Service, Service Quality, Easy
  Process, Easy Communication, Sales Support, Customer Support, Billing /
  Payment, Fuel Program Integration, Driver Convenience, Trust,
  Relationship With Truck.me Team, or Other.
- **Their main reason for staying with the service** — Better Price,
  Convenience, Locations, Shop Network, Service Quality, Fast Service,
  Discounts / Promotions, Easy Payment, Fuel Program, Relationship With
  Team, Trust, Driver Preference, No Better Alternative, or Other.
- **What could be improved** — pick everything the client mentions: Better
  Price, More Discounts, More Promotions, More Locations, Better Shops,
  Faster Service, Better Service Quality, Better Communication, Better
  Billing, More Services, Better Driver Experience, Better Support,
  Nothing, or Other.
- **Whether they have any concerns** — Yes or No.

**If they do have a concern**, you'll be asked what it's mainly about, and
a few extra questions will show up depending on your answer:

| Their main concern is...   | What else to capture                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Price**                  | What exactly about the price bothers them (too high, wants a bigger discount, promotion ended, doesn't feel it's worth it, unexpected fees, final price surprised them, budget limits, other). If it's that a competitor is cheaper, also note the competitor's name and roughly how much cheaper (under 5%, 5–10%, 11–20%, over 20%, or not sure). |
| **Competitor**             | Why they prefer the competitor (price, discounts, locations, coverage, service quality, speed, existing contract, credit/payment terms, relationship, driver or management preference, shop network, other), and the competitor's name.                                                                                                             |
| **Location / Coverage**    | What the actual location problem is (no shop on their route, none in the state/city they need, current locations inconvenient, their preferred shop isn't in network, driver can't reach a location, route changed, needs wider coverage, other) — plus the state and city/area they actually need, and any shop they'd suggest adding.             |
| **Service Availability**   | What went wrong with availability (not offered, shop was full, no appointment, parts unavailable, shop closed, long wait, took too long, service not supported, other).                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Bad Service Experience** | What went wrong (poor quality, long wait, bad attitude, incorrect repair, unresolved problem, unexpected charges, truck damaged, poor communication, driver or shop complaint, wouldn't return to that shop, other) — plus which service it was about, the shop's name, and how serious it was (Minor, Medium, Serious, Critical).                  |
| **Billing / Payment**      | What the billing issue is (wrong invoice, wrong or unexpected charge, payment method problem, credit issue, refund issue, open dispute, payment terms, process too complicated, other).                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Communication**          | What the communication issue is (doesn't understand the service, doesn't know what's available, wasn't kept informed, slow responses, poor sales or support communication, too many calls, an old issue never got resolved, doesn't know who to contact, other).                                                                                    |
| **Driver Related**         | What's going on with the driver (picks their own shop, prefers a competitor, doesn't know about the service or its locations, refuses or forgot to use it, had a bad experience, wasn't informed, other).                                                                                                                                           |

Whatever comes up, finish with the **next follow-up date**.

## Step 2d — Re-engagement calls

Use this when you're reaching out to a client who's stopped using the
service, to figure out why and whether there's a path back.

- **Why the client stopped** — pick the closest reason. If you genuinely
  don't know yet, pick **Unknown** — and come back and update this once you
  do find out, later in the same call or on a future one.

Depending on the reason, a few extra questions show up:

| Reason they stopped...                                           | What else to capture                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Price**                                                        | What exactly about the price was the problem (too high, wants a bigger discount, promotion ended, doesn't feel it's worth it, unexpected fees, final price surprised them, budget limits, other). If it's that a competitor is cheaper, also note the competitor's name and roughly how much cheaper (under 5%, 5–10%, 11–20%, over 20%, or not sure). |
| **Competitor**                                                   | Why they went with the competitor (price, discounts, locations, coverage, service quality, speed, existing contract, credit/payment terms, relationship, driver or management preference, shop network, other), and the competitor's name.                                                                                                             |
| **No Current Need**                                              | Why there's no need right now (service isn't due yet, low mileage, trucks temporarily inactive, low freight volume, seasonal business, rarely needs service, will need it later, timing unclear, other).                                                                                                                                               |
| **Internal Maintenance**                                         | How they're handling it themselves (own shop, own mechanics, does oil changes/PM/tires in-house, buys parts directly, company policy requires internal maintenance, other).                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Bad Service Experience**                                       | What went wrong (poor quality, long wait, bad attitude, incorrect repair, unresolved problem, unexpected charges, truck damaged, poor communication, driver or shop complaint, wouldn't return to that shop, other) — plus which service it was about, the shop's name, and how serious it was (Minor, Medium, Serious, Critical).                     |
| **Location / Coverage**                                          | What the actual location problem is (no shop on their route, none in the state/city they need, current locations inconvenient, their preferred shop isn't in network, driver can't reach a location, route changed, needs wider coverage, other) — plus the state and city/area they actually need, and any shop they'd suggest adding.                |
| **Service Availability**                                         | What went wrong with availability (not offered, shop was full, no appointment, parts unavailable, shop closed, long wait, took too long, service not supported, other).                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Billing / Payment**                                            | What the billing issue is (wrong invoice, wrong or unexpected charge, payment method problem, credit issue, refund issue, open dispute, payment terms, process too complicated, other).                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Driver Related**                                               | What's going on with the driver (picks their own shop, prefers a competitor, doesn't know about the service or its locations, refuses or forgot to use it, had a bad experience, wasn't informed, other).                                                                                                                                              |
| **Communication / Trust**                                        | What the underlying problem is (doesn't understand the service, doesn't know what's available, wasn't kept informed, doesn't trust the company or its shops, an old issue never got resolved, poor communication, no relationship with their agent, other).                                                                                            |
| **Company / Management**                                         | What changed on their end (decision maker changed, owner or fleet manager doesn't approve, dispatcher doesn't use the program, company policy changed, they're using a different vendor, account temporarily suspended, company was sold, other).                                                                                                      |
| **Business Slowdown**, **Fleet Reduced**, **Unknown**, **Other** | Nothing further needed — just the reason itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

Then, regardless of the reason, capture what it would actually take to win
the client back:

- **What can bring the client back** — pick everything that applies: better
  price, additional discount, a promotion, a better shop, a new location,
  better coverage, resolving a previous issue, better service quality,
  faster service, a billing resolution, better payment terms, a new
  service, explaining the program again, informing the drivers, contacting
  a new decision maker, better communication, better support, a sales
  follow-up, fuel sales support, the client needing service again, following
  up later, nothing currently, unknown, or other.
- **Which service(s)** you'd want to bring them back for — pick from the
  usual service list, or "Any Service."
- **Return potential** — how likely this client is to come back: High,
  Medium, Low, No Potential, or Unknown. This is just for prioritizing —
  it doesn't move the client anywhere on its own.
- **Re-engagement status** — where things currently stand: New, In
  Progress, Waiting For Client, Waiting For Truck.me Action, Service
  Expected, Follow-up Later, or Ready To Close. Update this every time you
  touch base, so anyone glancing at the record knows where things stand
  without reading every past call.

**Most re-engagement calls end here** — you're just checking in and moving
the status forward. Only if this call actually **wraps up** the
re-engagement effort (client's back, or you're done trying) do you answer
one more question:

- **Closing out this re-engagement?** — Yes or No. Leave it No for a normal
  check-in call.

**If Yes**, pick the **outcome**, and — depending on which one — a bit more
detail:

| Outcome                                | What else to capture                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reactivated**                        | What actually brought them back — pick everything that applies (better price, discount, promotion, better shop, new location, better coverage, previous issue resolved, better service quality, faster service, billing issue resolved, better payment terms, new service, better communication, better support, sales follow-up, fuel sales support, client needed service again, other). Only use this outcome when the client has genuinely used a service again — a promise or renewed interest isn't enough on its own. |
| **Still Working**                      | Nothing extra — just keep the Re-engagement Status above up to date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Not Now**                            | Why not right now (service not needed yet, low mileage, seasonal business, trucks temporarily inactive, budget, waiting for approval, waiting for better timing, business slowdown, other).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Unable To Reach**                    | Nothing else to fill in — picking this just marks the client as unreachable for now. This is not the same as the client refusing or churning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Lost To Competitor**                 | The competitor's name, and why they won (better price, discounts, locations, coverage, service quality, speed, credit/payment terms, existing contract or relationship, driver or management preference, other).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Hard Refusal** / **Out Of Business** | Nothing else to fill in here — see below.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Other**                              | Nothing extra required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

**Reactivated, Still Working, Not Now, and Unable To Reach** end the call
the normal way: fill in the next follow-up date and submit.

**Lost To Competitor, Hard Refusal, and Out Of Business are different** —
picking one of these means the client is being marked as lost, not just
paused. You won't be asked for a next follow-up date here, and nothing is
saved yet when you submit — instead, the widget takes you straight into the
**Churned form** (below) to finish the intake. Everything from this call is
saved together with that form as a single record once you submit there.
Anything you already entered (like the competitor's name) carries over
automatically so you won't be asked twice; just double check it and fill in
the rest.

## Step 2e — Churned calls

You can land here two ways: by picking **Churned Feedback** directly from
the first screen, or automatically right after a Re-engagement call ends in
Lost To Competitor, Hard Refusal, or Out Of Business (see above) — in that
case some fields will already be filled in for you.

Use this only when there's a real, solid reason to consider the client
lost — not just because it's been a while since they used a service. If
it's just gone quiet, that's a Dormant client, not a Churned one; there's
no separate form for that distinction here, so use your judgment about
whether Churned is really warranted before you pick it.

- **Churn Reason** — why the client is gone: Price, Lost To Competitor, Bad
  Service Experience, No Current Need, Internal Maintenance, Location /
  Coverage, Service Availability, Billing / Payment, Communication / Trust,
  Driver Related, Company / Management, Fleet Reduced, Business Slowdown,
  Business Closed, Hard Refusal, or Other.

Depending on the reason, a few extra questions show up — these are the same
questions asked in the Re-engagement form, so if you've filled these out
there before, they'll look familiar:

| Reason...                                                                                  | What else to capture                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Price**                                                                                  | What exactly about the price was the problem (too high, wants a bigger discount, promotion ended, doesn't feel it's worth it, unexpected fees, final price surprised them, budget limits, other). If it's that a competitor is cheaper, also note the competitor's name and roughly how much cheaper. |
| **Lost To Competitor**                                                                     | The competitor's name, and why they won (better price, discounts, locations, coverage, service quality, speed, credit/payment terms, existing contract or relationship, driver or management preference, other).                                                                                      |
| **Bad Service Experience**                                                                 | What went wrong, plus which service it was about, the shop's name, and how serious it was.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **No Current Need**                                                                        | Why there's no need right now (service isn't due yet, low mileage, trucks temporarily inactive, low freight volume, seasonal business, rarely needs service, will need it later, timing unclear, other).                                                                                              |
| **Internal Maintenance**                                                                   | How they're handling it themselves.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Location / Coverage**                                                                    | What the actual location problem is, plus the state and city/area they actually needed, and any shop they'd have suggested adding.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Service Availability**                                                                   | What went wrong with availability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Billing / Payment**                                                                      | What the billing issue was.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Communication / Trust**                                                                  | What the underlying problem was.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Driver Related**                                                                         | What's going on with the driver.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Company / Management**                                                                   | What changed on their end.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Fleet Reduced**, **Business Slowdown**, **Business Closed**, **Hard Refusal**, **Other** | Nothing further needed — just the reason itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

Then, always:

- **Lost Services** — which service(s) the client is no longer using: pick
  from the usual service list, "All Services," or Other.
- **Return Possibility** — how likely this client is to ever come back:
  High, Medium, Low, No Chance, or Unknown. This is just for prioritizing —
  it doesn't start any separate process on its own. If you arrived here
  from a Re-engagement call, this is pre-filled from the return potential
  you rated there (double check it still feels right — you can change it).

**If Return Possibility is High, Medium, or Low**, one more question shows
up:

- **What could bring the client back?** — pick everything that applies:
  better price, additional discount, a promotion, a better shop, a new
  location, better coverage, resolving a previous issue, better service
  quality, faster service, a billing resolution, better payment terms, a
  new service, better communication, better support, a new decision maker,
  fuel sales support, relationship recovery, the business becoming active
  again, or other. If you arrived here from a Re-engagement call, you'll
  see a reminder of what was said to that question there — it's not
  copied in automatically since the options aren't quite the same list,
  but worth a glance before you answer fresh.

If Return Possibility is **No Chance**, this question is skipped entirely —
there's nothing more to plan for.

Finish with the **next follow-up date**, same as every other form.

## A few things to keep in mind

- Every call you log always ends with a next follow-up date — even a quick
  "no answer" needs one, so nothing falls through the cracks.
- Once you submit, you'll see a confirmation message. After that, the
  widget closes and the page refreshes on its own — you don't need to do
  anything else.
